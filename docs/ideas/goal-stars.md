# Goal stars

Status: idea.

## What it does

A small yellow pixel star on each calendar day where the daily goal was
reached.

## Why it is interesting

The calendar shades show how much was drunk, but not which days hit the goal.
A star makes the good days stand out, matching the star burst when the goal is
reached.

## What it would touch

How calendar days are drawn, and what is stored per day.

## Open questions

- Which goal counts for a past day: the daily goal at the time, which means
  storing each day's goal, or today's daily goal.
